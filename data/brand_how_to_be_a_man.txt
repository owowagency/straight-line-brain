title: How To Be A Man — Principes

Principes door Dusan Djukich:

- Never apologize for living, how others won't live.
- Say who you will be, and follow through.
- Say what you will do, and follow through.
- Don't stop until the job is done.
- When in doubt, always kiss the girl.
- Don't use words like safe space, or psychological safety. You will instantly lose credibility.
- Don't insult people in front of their children.
- Be proactive.
- Don't force others to drag you along for your own good.
- Always own what's in front of you. That includes your case.
- Never split a check.
- Take your tiredness with you, while doing what you said you would do.
- The weak look at cost. Those who thrive, look at investment opportunity.
- Never negotiate with yourself. Just don't do it.
- Stop focusing on being liked, and start focusing on being effective.
- Stop talking about the good old days. They're dead.
- Ignore unsolicited feedback. It's almost always for the sender.
- Bracket the impulse to be nice. Especially, when you're not sincere.
- Never cancel plans for dinner by text message.
- Show up on time, and leave early.
- It's okay to forgive, just don't be stupid enough to forget.
- Don't keep consuming something just because it's free.
- Raw, intelligent action will always look good on you.
- Close your mouth while listening to others.
- Acting passionate, is not a workable substitute for generating adequate results.
- If you want something, ask for it. Don't spend time beating around the bush.
- Whining is always seen as weak. Just don't do it.
- Never respond to a compliment with a compliment. Just say, "Thank you."
- The number one source of failure, is obeying weak thoughts.
- Groveling for acceptance is the surest way to get your vote canceled. Show respect for yourself.
- Don't virtue signal. Intelligent people know what you're up to.
- Know that people will forgive rudeness and cruelty, long before they will forgive weakness.
- Insist that others keep their agreements with you.
- Stop reacting and start responding. Reaction is emotional. Response is strategic.
- No matter how destitute you are, never act poor.
- Each time you distract yourself with nonsense, you die a little more.
- Hold your drink at waist level.
- Don't get reasonable and back down. You will live to regret it.
- Since you're always leaking, leak strength, and not weakness.
- Don't blame others, for you not having a life, that you did not have the courage to create for yourself.
