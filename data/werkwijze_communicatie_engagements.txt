title: Types of Communication Engagements

De drie communicatiestijlen corresponderen met de drie werelden (Cirkel, Zigzag, Straight-Line) en bepalen hoe effectief je communiceert.

## Straight-Line Communicatie
- Speaking FOR the person, as the creator of their life.
- Laser-like focus.
- Speaking it and not around it.
- Highly intentional.
- Generative language in use.
- Extends conversations.
- Actively duplicates other's communications.
- Actively listens for what is not being said.
- Appropriate tonality.
- Expanded awareness of public identity.
- Brackets case and unworkable IWD effectively.
- High Status energy.
- Clear on endgame.
- Proficient at connecting the dots.

## Circle Communicatie
- Lacks clarity on endgame.
- Low Status energy.
- Excessive descriptive language in use.
- Excessive explanations.
- Tends to be dull and boring.
- Unfocused.
- Low awareness of public identity.
- Little, if any, bracketing of case or unworkable IWD.
- Repeats self constantly.

## Zigzag Communicatie
- Bull in a china shop.
- Lacks endgame clarity.
- Erratic energy patterns.
- Distractive behaviors and actions.
- Unfocused.
- Difficult for others to follow.
- Often jerky movements and desperate tonality.
- Lacks the discipline to slow down and deal with what's in front of him/her.
- Doesn't hold space well.
