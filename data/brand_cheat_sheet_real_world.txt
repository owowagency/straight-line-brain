title: A Quick Cheat Sheet — How to Navigate the Real World

Principes voor het navigeren van de echte wereld, door Dusan Djukich:

- Get competent, at something others experience as valuable.
- Drive, owns positive thinking.
- If people feel they are better off with you, than without you, they will usually stay.
- Be generous whenever you can.
- Say what you are going to do, and follow through.
- Ignore feelings, and focus on facts.
- Define your terms, and ask others to define their terms, as well.
- Don't justify unworkable behavior.
- People don't care what you believe, they care about what you actually do.
- Humans are wired to seek certainty.
- Constantly shun reasonableness wherever you find it.
- Never allow the game to get bigger than you.
- If you show up with low energy, you are doomed to failure.
- Always be willing to fight back.
- Power does not respect politeness, it respects posture.
- Never trade your work ethic, for another person's pleasant personality.
- Don't deal with anxiety through food.
- Destroy your weakness.
- People often lie, about how good things are, and about how bad things are.
- Remember, that it is human nature, to take advantage of the weak and stupid.
- Know, that there is a dominance hierarchy, and you are somewhere on it.
- Realize, most people don't love you, but they do love, what they think you can do for them.
- Artificially constructed closeness, is a scam strategy, designed to distract you.
- Keep moving in straight lines.
- Morality isn't truth, it's strategy.
- A projected, kind and loving act, often conceals how bitter and nasty someone can be.
- Complaining, and excessive explanations, show up as annoying and weak.
- Calling a lie, a commitment, doesn't make it a commitment.
- Endurance, is often more useful than intelligence.
- Never wait, for others to come along and save you.
- Know that most people, are never as helpless as they pretend to be.
- Stay hungry.
